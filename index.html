A different kind of advisory

We are a consulting partner, not just an advisor.

Sound judgment matters. So does being there when the work begins.

Local Startup 28 helps turn entrepreneurial potential into operating businesses. We work with aspiring founders who have a clear idea but need a plan, connections or capital — and with investors who are ready to invest but want a considered business direction.

Our role is to connect ambition with the practical conditions for success: a validated opportunity, an operational roadmap, trusted local relationships and the right people around the table. We advise with rigor, and we remain an execution partner as plans become real.

Independent thinking. Accountable partnership. — Local Startup 28
How it works

A clear plan from idea to launch.

Every business is different. We learn about your goals, how much you can invest and what customers in your area need. Then we plan the next steps with you.

We support you from your first idea until your business opens.
01

Discovery Call

We talk about your business idea, what you already have to get started, any problems you may face and what people in your area need.

02

Strategy & Roadmap

We check if your idea can work, make a plan that fits the money you can invest and decide what to do next.

03

Execution Support

We help you find the right people and businesses to work with, plan how to get the money you need, and organize daily work.

04

Launch & Grow

We help you open your business, listen to your customers and improve it as it grows.
